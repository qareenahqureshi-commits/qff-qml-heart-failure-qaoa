The problem

Most risk tools assume a hospital that has everything.

The strongest heart-failure marker, ejection fraction, needs an echocardiogram: a machine and a trained sonographer. Many clinics have neither, but they all have a blood test.

The quantum engine

Panel selection is a QUBO. QAOA solves it.

  • One qubit per candidate measurement. The score rewards tests that separate deaths from survivors and penalises overlap between them; test prices are exact extra terms.
  • QAOA in Qiskit, 11 qubits, depth 1 to 3, 2000 shots. With INTERP starting angles, 28 % of shots are the optimal panel: 46 times random.
  • On a simulated IBM Heron chip with real calibration noise, depth p = 1 works best, so that is what we run on hardware.

Why quantum, honestly. At 11 measurements brute force can check every answer, and QAOA matched it in every run. That check is what lets us trust it on 50 to 500 candidate markers, where brute force is impossible. We do not claim quantum advantage at 11: simulated annealing solves it in 0.3 s.
